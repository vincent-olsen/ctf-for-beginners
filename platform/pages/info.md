# Capture The Flag

Welcome to an afternoon of legal hacking, pizza and beer.

**When:** see the countdown on the front page.

In a CTF you solve small hacking challenges, and the goal is to find a hidden flag in the format `flag{...}`. Paste it into the challenge, and your team gets the points. Never done this before? No worries, the intro talk covers the essentials, and it will all make sense.

---

## Start here
- **Your first challenge:** start with **meow1**. Want to try pwn? **overflow1** is the gentle way in.
- **Tools worth having ready:**
  - `nc` (netcat), to connect to the challenges that give you `nc <host> <port>`. On Windows, use `ncat` from [Nmap](https://nmap.org/download.html).
  - [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/), to decode, decrypt and convert right in the browser.
  - Your browser's developer tools (F12), for the web challenges: the page source, network requests and cookies.

---

## How it works
- Every challenge hides a flag, e.g. `flag{n0e_rand0m_t3xt}`. If a challenge uses another format, it says so.
- Upper or lower case doesn't matter.
- Stuck? There are hints. They cost a few points (100 → a 10-point hint → 90), so use them wisely.
- Googling is allowed. That's how it's done in real life too.

---

## Two lanes
- **Classic:** no AI. Just Google, docs and your own head. Honour system.
- **AI-modus:** AI expected. Much harder challenges, so bring your assistant.

Each lane has its own scoreboard. Switch between them at the top of the page.

---

## Categories
- **Crypto:** encryption and encoded messages. Crack them.
- **Forensics:** dig up what's hidden in files, images and other traces.
- **Web:** find the holes in websites and APIs.
- **Pwn:** make a program do something it really shouldn't.
- **Misc:** everything else, where a bit of creativity helps.

---

## Teams
The idea is teams of two. If you absolutely want to be a loner, that's allowed too, but winning gets harder. And yes, there will be a prize 👀

One person creates the team; the others join with the team name and password.

---

## Rules
- Collect as many points as possible.
- Don't share flags with other teams.
- Only attack the challenge hosts listed in the CTF. Never other players, and never the CTF website itself: it isn't one of the challenges :)
- No brute force against the servers or the flag field, and no DoS. Cracking a file you downloaded, on your own machine, is fine.
- Don't break files or services, the others want to play too.

---

## Help
- Ask the organisers for help, whether a challenge seems broken or you're wondering about anything else.
- Found a hole in the CTF website itself? Don't use it, tell the organisers.
